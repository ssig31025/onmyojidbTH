Time Rift Rotation

Active
The tides of battle turn. Exclusive effect. At the start of the battle, Taira no Masakado's starting ATK exceeds 700% DEF, enters Offensive Stance; otherwise enters Defensive Stance. Wields artifact Futsunomitama. [Cast] Switches stance. Offensive Stance (攻击架势) : When casting Eternal Blazing Sun, enhances Futsunomitama once. Defensive Stance (防守架势) : When attacked, recovers HP equal to 500% of starting DEF (up to 70% of starting ATK) and enhances Futsunomitama once (can be enhanced once per turn). Futsunomitama : After every 3 enhancements, Taira no Masakado immediately enters Rift in Time.
Orb Cost: 1
Cooldown: 0 turns
Level Up:
  • Lv.2: After ends, reduces own duration of received and by 1 turn.
  • Lv.3: While in , Eternal Blazing Sun's orb cost is reduced by 2.
  • Lv.4: Defensive Stance's effect also applies to other allies, with the amount equal to 70% of their own stats (does not trigger when affected by ).
  • Lv.5: While in Offensive Stance, each time Futsunomitama is enhanced, Eternal Blazing Sun's AoE multiplier increases by 5% (max 15%).

Eternal Blazing Sun

Active
Halting the war with my might, no legion's power can compare to mine. Taira no Masakado attacks all enemies 3 times, dealing 64% ATK damage each hit. Then he deals ruin damage equal to 18% of total damage dealt to selected enemy. If an enemy is defeated, gains 3 orbs and strengthens Futsunomitama once. Ruin Damage: One of the types of damage, ignores DEF and damage reduction effects. Futsunomitama : After every 3 enhancements, Taira no Masakado immediately enters Rift in Time.
Orb Cost: 3
Cooldown: 0 turns
Level Up:
  • Lv.2: Increases AoE damage to 68%.
  • Lv.3: Increases AoE damage to 72%.
  • Lv.4: Increases AoE damage to 76%.
  • Lv.5: AoE damage increases to 80%; for each enemy defeated, receives 7% less damage and ruin damage coefficient increases by 5% (up to 5 times).
